REGION

Kan Tatlisi

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Desert Kan Tatlisi is a vast, rocky region dominated by reddish soil, iron-rich sands, and hard exposed ground. Despite its appearance, it is not a hot desert. The climate is generally dry and harsh, with temperatures varying considerably between day and night and across the seasons.

Much of the landscape consists of broad stretches of red earth broken by pale and dark rock formations, gravel fields, and scattered ridges. The ground is often hard and uneven, making travel difficult outside established routes. Vegetation is sparse across much of the region, growing wherever enough moisture collects in cracks, shallow depressions, and sheltered areas.

Open grasslands occur sporadically between the rocky expanses, usually around areas with more reliable access to water. These patches can support considerably more wildlife and may become important seasonal gathering places for Radrian clans.

Thick, water-storing trees are scattered throughout the region, particularly around lakes, oases, and underground water sources. Their dark reddish to nearly black foliage stands out against the red ground and pale stone. Some of these trees can survive for long periods without rainfall by storing large amounts of water within their trunks and extensive root systems.

Several lakes and isolated oases provide rare permanent or semi-permanent sources of water. These locations support dense pockets of vegetation and attract animals from across the surrounding desert, making them important ecological and social landmarks. Many Radrian travel routes naturally follow the chain of reliable water sources scattered throughout Kan Tatlisi.

Konika

Konika is a broad grassland region lying north of Kan Tatlisi, separated from the desert by a rugged mountain range. The region is noticeably more fertile and supports much denser vegetation, with wide stretches of grassland broken by scattered trees, rocky outcrops, and shallow valleys.

The landscape contains considerably more limestone than Kan Tatlisi, producing pale cliffs, exposed rock shelves, and light-coloured ridges throughout the region. Limestone formations are particularly common around the mountain foothills and waterways, where erosion has shaped the terrain into terraces, ravines, and natural shelters.

Although Konika receives more moisture than the lands to the south, its vegetation remains adapted to Esterasul's low-light conditions. The grasslands are dominated by reddish, dark purple, and almost black grasses, while trees tend to have thick trunks and deep root systems that allow them to retain water. During wetter periods, vegetation can spread across large areas of the plains.

The mountains separating Konika from Kan Tatlisi are difficult to cross, but they contain a natural route known as Tazar ǂarun, meaning "mountain pass." The passage provides one of the most practical routes between the two regions and has historically been important for Radrian migration, travel, and trade.

Beyond the pass, the landscape gradually opens into the expansive grasslands of Konika. The contrast between the red, rocky terrain of Kan Tatlisi and the more heavily vegetated limestone plains of Konika makes the mountain range a distinct ecological boundary between the two regions.
