LEGACY DESIGNS

The Rex Radrian

Rex is a former Radrian subspecies, known for its distinctive crystal-like horns. It is now closed to new characters.

Closed legacy subspecies • Crystal-like horns • Predates the open species
＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Before Radrians became the open species they are today, I created characters that would eventually become part of the species' history. Among these early designs were the Rex Radrians.

Rex is a former Radrian subspecies characterized primarily by its distinctive crystal-like horns, which could appear in unnatural colours.

Unlike the three current subspecies, Rex is no longer open for new characters. The existing Rex characters are legacy characters belonging to the creator and remain part of the history of Radrians.

BEFORE RADRIANS WERE RADRIANS

The Rex designs predate the modern Radrian species. They were created as characters before Radrians existed as an established species, meaning they were not originally designed under the same rules and structure used by the current open species.

As Radrians developed into a defined species with established anatomy, biology and design guidelines, some of these older concepts no longer fit the direction of the species. Rather than erasing them, they became part of its history.

Rex is therefore a legacy subspecies. It represents an earlier stage of the project rather than a current design path.

LEGACY DESIGNS

What Is a Legacy Design?

An older character or concept that belongs to the history of Radrians but does not follow the current species guidelines.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

A legacy design is an older character or concept that belongs to the history of Radrians but does not necessarily follow the current species guidelines.

Legacy designs can preserve ideas, characters and artwork from earlier stages of development without making those elements available for new character creation.

Think of them as pieces of the species' past that are still part of its story. They can still be drawn, referenced, archived and appreciated. They simply aren't something new creators can reproduce as official Radrians.

◉
REX

The Rex Subspecies

Rex Radrians were distinguished by unusual, crystalline horns in colours that are not natural for modern Radrians.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Rex Radrians were distinguished from the current Radrian subspecies by their unusual horn structures. Their horns were notably crystalline in appearance and could feature colours that would not normally be considered natural Radrian coloration.

This is one of the major reasons they don't fit within the current design guidelines. Modern Radrians are intentionally kept relatively grounded in their biology and appearance, with natural coloration and recognizable anatomical traits being important parts of the species identity.

Rex belongs to an earlier creative era of the project.

REX

Can I Make a Rex Radrian?

No. Rex is a closed legacy subspecies. New creators should use Southern, Mountain or Forest.

Closed • No plans to reopen • Not a temporary MYO restriction
＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

No. Rex is a closed legacy subspecies.

New creators should use one of the three currently open subspecies: Southern, Mountain or Forest.

The existing species rules specifically state that new Rex Radrians cannot be created. There are currently no plans to reopen Rex for new character creation.

This isn't a temporary MYO restriction or a limited event. Rex is simply not part of the current open species lineup.

✧
REX

Existing Rex Characters

Existing Rex characters remain valid as legacy characters and do not need to be redesigned.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Existing Rex characters remain valid as legacy characters. They don't need to be redesigned into one of the modern subspecies. They don't need to be removed from the world. They don't need to stop being part of Radrian history.

Their existence simply reflects the fact that Radrians have changed and developed over time. The current species is built upon years of previous artwork, characters and ideas, and those older designs are part of that history.

HISTORY

Why Keep Them?

Because not everything needs to be erased when a species evolves.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Radrians have gone through multiple stages of development. Some concepts were abandoned. Some were redesigned. Some became canon. Some became obsolete. And some simply remained as old characters.

Rex represents one of those earlier stages. Keeping these designs allows the species to have a visible history rather than pretending it appeared fully formed one day.

RULES

Legacy Does Not Mean Available

Existing Rex characters can continue to exist and be drawn. New Rex characters cannot be created.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

This distinction is important.

EXISTING REX CHARACTERS

  • - Can continue to exist
  • - Can continue to be drawn
  • - Can remain part of their owner's character collection
  • - Can remain part of Radrian history
  • - Can be referenced as examples of the species' earlier development

NEW REX CHARACTERS

  • - Cannot be created
  • - Cannot be adopted as a new Rex design
  • - Cannot be submitted as a new Radrian subspecies
  • - Are not part of the current open species

OPEN SUBSPECIES

The Three Current Subspecies

If you want to create a Radrian today, these are the available options.

Southern 330-370 cm • Mountain 340-390 cm • Forest 290-330 cm
＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

If you want to create a Radrian today, these are the available options:

  • Southern (330-370 cm): desert-adapted, slender, smaller horns, craftsmanship
  • Mountain (340-390 cm): largest subspecies, powerful build, massive horns, mountainous habitats
  • Forest (290-330 cm): smallest subspecies, compact build, northern forests, distinctive horns

These are the current open Radrian subspecies.

HISTORY

A Window Into the Past

Rex Radrians aren't a hidden fourth option. They're a snapshot of where Radrians came from.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Rex Radrians aren't meant to be a hidden fourth option. They're a snapshot of where Radrians came from.

The species has changed. The designs have changed. The world has changed. And that's okay.

The old characters remain as they were, little pieces of the project's earliest history, preserved alongside the much larger world Radrians have become today.

You cannot create a new Rex Radrian. But you can still look back at them.
