A Note on Regional Dialects

While every clan speaks Radric, pronunciation and a handful of words shift by region — Tundra clans soften the rolled rr almost to silence, while Sunreach clans clip vowels shorter in the heat. None of this is standardised, and keepers are welcome to invent small dialect quirks for their own Radrians.
