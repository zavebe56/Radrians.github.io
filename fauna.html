Fauna of Esterasul

Radrians share every region of Esterasul with other wildlife — some predators, some prey, some cultivated alongside the clans. This archive expands as new species are recorded.

Grasslands · Domesticated · Carnivore · Pet

Totogu

Medium sized avitherapsid that resemble a lizard

Size: dog-sized · Diet: carnivore · Relationship: domesticated
+ Read full entry Collapse entry

Totogus are medium avitherapsids that resemble lizards. They're covered in short protofeathers with few longer quills sticking out of their neck and back. Their intelligence and behavior is similar to a dog and they're a domesticated species that's often seen with Radrians. Their coats can come in various patterns and colors, their tongues are always blue.

Grasslands & Domesticated · Omnivore · Pet

Takebi

A small omnivore kept as pet and pest control.

Size: cat-sized · Diet: omnivore · Relationship: domesticated
+ Read full entry Collapse entry

Takebis are small omnivores that are domesticated pets similar to Totogu. Takebis are good at pest control and they enjoy a close company of radrians. They have short, strong beak and their plumage can come in various colors and patterns. Their feet have hooves and they use them to dig in the ground to find food.

Deve Kutsu

Deve Kutsu is a big omnivore that is domesticated and suitable for a mount.

Size: large, slow · Diet: omnivore · Relationship: revered, unbothered

Deve Kutsu is a big omnivore that is domesticated and suitable for a mount. They have two subspecies in different sizes so they're both suitable for rides for radrians and humans. On the graphic is bigger subspecies. The smaller one have less reddish plumage. Males have bright blue nose and females have it a bit smaller and not blue.

Grasslands · Herbivore

Vellopi

frog

Size: hand-sized · Diet: herbivore · Relationship: foraged food source
+ Read full entry Collapse entry

A small, toad-like herbivore that thrives in damp grasslands and marshy areas. Vellopi have short, sturdy limbs, making them slow movers, but their exceptional camouflage keeps them safe from predators. Rather than running, they rely on stillness and subtle swaying to mimic the movement of surrounding grass. They feed on soft plants, moss, and fungi, using their hard beaks. When disturbed, they freeze in place, blending into their surroundings.
