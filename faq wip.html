FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Answers to the questions we get most about the archive, the Radrians, and how to use this site.

BEFORE YOU DIG IN

If you're new to the archive, start here. These are the questions most visitors ask before they get lost in the SPECIES and WORLD sections — what this project actually is, how the archive is organized, and how to get in touch if you've spotted something we missed.

What is Esterasul? +

Esterasul is the ongoing field archive for the Radrian species — a running record of their subspecies, anatomy, language, habitat, and the animals that share their world. Think of it as a living reference rather than a finished book: entries get revised as new field notes come in.

What exactly are the Radrians? +

The short answer lives on the SPECIES page. In brief, they're the subject species of this archive, documented across several known subspecies with their own anatomy and dialects — start there for the overview, then branch into SUBSPECIES, ANATOMY, or LANGUAGE for detail.

Where should I start if I'm new here? +

The homepage's quick-link row (OVERVIEW, SUBSPECIES, ANATOMY, WORLD, FAUNA, GALLERY) is the fastest way in. If you want the full narrative arc, read SPECIES first, then WORLD for context on where they live, then FAUNA for what lives alongside them.

How often is the archive updated? +

New entries are added as discoveries are confirmed rather than on a fixed schedule. The DISCOVERIES page lists the most recent additions first, so that's the page to check if you want to see what's changed lately.

Can I submit a discovery or correction? +

Yes — reach out through the contact details on the DISCOVERIES page with what you observed, where, and any supporting images. Submissions are reviewed before they're folded into the archive, so entries stay consistent with existing records.

Can I reuse images or text from the archive? +

Get in touch first. Some material comes from outside contributors and carries its own terms, so we handle reuse requests case by case rather than granting blanket permission.
