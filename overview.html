OVERVIEW

Radrians

What began as a private setting eventually became an open species, a project where other artists could create their own Radrians, tell their own stories, and contribute their own ideas.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Radrians are a sapient species of tall, animal-like humanoids created by Raksz.

They were originally created as part of a personal worldbuilding project around 2014 and have continued to grow and change ever since. What began as a private setting eventually became an open species, a project where other artists could create their own Radrians, tell their own stories, and contribute their own ideas.

Radrians have their own biology, cultures, languages, creatures and history, but you don't have to use all of it.

You can place a Radrian in Esterasul. You can place one in a fantasy kingdom. You can put one in a modern city, a sci-fi universe, a D&D campaign, or a completely original setting.

The original world is an invitation, not a restriction.

OPEN SPECIES

What Is an Open Species?

An open species is a species that anyone can create characters of, following the species' basic design rules. No permission, MYO or approval needed.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

An open species is a species that anyone can create characters of, following the species' basic design rules.

You don't need permission to make a Radrian. You don't need to buy a MYO. You don't need to submit your character for approval.

You can create personal characters, stories, artwork, adoptables and your own interpretations of the species.

You can even create Radrian characters outside of their original setting. The original species FAQ explicitly allows Radrians to be adapted to different genres and worlds, including settings with magic or completely different technology.

The basic idea is simple: learn the species, design your character, make something of your own. That's it.

A COLLABORATIVE WORLD

The original world of Radrians is not intended to be something that only one person can ever add to. The project has always encouraged the community to create: new characters, new creatures, new artwork, new interpretations, new stories, new places and new ideas.

From the start the idea was simple. Everyone can make their own characters, creatures, legends and original stories. This world was meant to be created by many hands.

You don't have to be an experienced worldbuilder. You don't have to be a professional artist. You don't even have to know everything about Radrians. Start with a character. Give them a personality. Give them a home. Give them a story. And if you want to go further, you can help expand the world around them.

THE ONLY REAL RULE

Make them recognizable as Radrians.

Radrians have distinctive anatomy and biological characteristics that make them what they are. You are free to experiment with patterns, accessories, clothing, hairstyles, body modifications, occupations, personalities, settings and stories. But the underlying species should still be recognizable.

The design rules exist to give Radrians their identity, not to limit what you can do with them.

THE WORLD

Esterasul Isn't the Only Possibility

Radrians originate from Esterasul, a harsh world of cold deserts, mountains, forests and scattered human settlements, but you don't have to use it at all.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Radrians originate from Esterasul, a harsh world of cold deserts, mountains, forests and scattered human settlements. Esterasul is not a gentle planet.

Water is precious. Temperatures can be extreme. Vast deserts stretch between mountain ranges, while forests and highlands create isolated regions with their own climates and ecosystems.

The native Radrian populations have adapted to this environment in very different ways. Some travel seasonally across the deserts. Some live among mountains. Others inhabit dense northern forests.

Human settlements exist alongside them, creating trade, cultural exchange, conflict and cooperation. The result is a world that is constantly changing.

BUT ESTERASUL ISN'T THE ONLY POSSIBILITY

The original Radrian project has always been intentionally flexible. It was established long ago that Radrians could exist in medieval fantasy, futuristic science-fiction, modern settings and other universes.

Your Radrian can be a wandering hunter, a farmer, a soldier, a scholar, a merchant, an adventurer, a mechanic, a wizard, a spaceship captain, a D&D character or a citizen of your own world.

Their species identity stays Radrian. Everything around them can change.

DESIGNING

Create Your Own Radrian

Start with the species. Then make them yours. The silhouette, colours, horns, ears and tail give Radrians their identity.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Designing a Radrian is meant to be flexible. There is no single "correct" personality, occupation, outfit or aesthetic. A Radrian can be elegant or rugged, quiet or energetic, friendly or intimidating, traditional or futuristic, covered in handmade jewellery or wearing nothing but practical clothing. The character is yours.

The most important part is understanding the biological foundation of the species.

THE RADRIAN SILHOUETTE

A Radrian should immediately look tall, elongated and distinctly animal-like. Their proportions are very different from a human's. They generally have a long neck, elongated limbs, long digitigrade legs, raised heels, a long tail, broad, squared shoulders, a strong neck, an elongated pelvis and an overall lean but powerful build.

Despite their long proportions, Radrians should not look fragile. Their body is built for endurance and movement. Think of a silhouette that feels somewhere between a creature and an athletic humanoid. Elegant. Strange. Powerful.

COLOUR & PATTERNS

Radrians have natural-looking coloration. Their appearance can be inspired by real animals, particularly mammals, birds and other creatures that could plausibly produce similar colours and patterns.

Stripes, spots, patches, gradients and other natural markings can all be used. You don't need to copy an existing animal. The goal is simply to avoid making the character look artificially neon or biologically implausible unless your particular setting gives you a reason to do so.

HORNS

The structures on a Radrian's forehead may look like horns, but their biology is unusual. They function primarily as thermoregulatory organs, containing extensive blood vessels and nerves. Heat can be transferred through these structures, helping Radrians regulate their body temperature.

Because of this, they are not simply decorative antlers. Their shape can vary between individuals and subspecies, but they should retain the basic Radrian structure.

EARS

Radrian ear shape is somewhat variable too. Their shape can be influenced by their subspecies, ancestry and individual genetics. Some may have small rounded ears. Others may have large, prominent ears. Different ear shapes can help make individual characters visually distinct while still keeping the recognizable Radrian head structure.

THE TAIL

The tail is an important part of the Radrian silhouette. It is long and substantial, with a thicker base and a tapering end. It helps with balance, particularly because Radrians stand on raised heels and have an elongated body.

The tail can also communicate emotion and body language. A relaxed tail, a tense tail, a defensive posture or a curious movement can all tell part of a character's story.

CLOTHING & ACCESSORIES

There are essentially no limits to the aesthetic direction you can take. Traditional clothing, modern clothes, fantasy armour, cybernetic modifications and jewellery are all welcome.

Radrians are especially associated with craftsmanship, ornamentation, embroidery and metalwork within their original culture. This makes accessories particularly useful for characters who originate from Esterasul. However, you don't have to follow Radrian culture when designing a character.

DESIGN FREEDOM

You can change almost everything that isn't fundamental biology: personalities, body types, clothing styles, cultures, occupations, settings, hairstyles, markings, accessories and stories.

A Radrian doesn't need to look like a character from Esterasul. They simply need to still look like a Radrian.

ANATOMY

The Radrian Body

Strange by human standards. Completely natural to them. Radrians are not humans with animal ears.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Radrians are not humans with animal ears. Their anatomy developed independently and should be treated as its own biological system. They are tall, warm-blooded, sapient creatures adapted to difficult environments. Their bodies combine characteristics reminiscent of mammals, reptiles and more primitive synapsid-like animals.

A LONG-BODIED SPECIES

The Radrian body is elongated from head to tail. Their neck is long. Their limbs are long. Their pelvis is elongated and angled forward. Their tail extends behind the body. Their shoulders are comparatively broad, creating a distinctive contrast between their narrow waist and powerful upper body.

This gives them their characteristic silhouette: long, upright, athletic and animal-like.

DIGITIGRADE LEGS

Radrians stand on the balls of their feet rather than placing their entire foot flat on the ground. Their heels remain raised, giving their legs a characteristic bent, digitigrade posture.

When drawing a Radrian, avoid giving them completely human legs with an ordinary flat-footed stance. The raised heel is one of the details that immediately changes their silhouette.

HANDS & FEET

Their hands and feet are elongated. Radrians do not naturally possess conventional mammalian claws or nails. Their fingertips are instead protected by hard keratinized structures.

Their feet retain a somewhat unusual evolutionary history, with anatomical characteristics that reflect their distant ancestry. They are capable of fine manipulation and craftsmanship despite their animal-like proportions. This is particularly important culturally: Radrians are skilled makers, engravers, embroiderers and metalworkers.

COAT

The Radrian coat is best understood as protofeathers rather than ordinary mammalian fur. The short coat can appear sleek and shiny, while longer growth can form a mane around areas such as the head, neck and tail.

This gives Radrians their characteristic feather-like appearance without turning them into birds. Their coat is functional rather than purely decorative.

HORNS THAT AREN'T QUITE HORNS

The structures on the forehead are one of the stranger features of Radrian biology. They contain extensive blood vessels and help with heat regulation. Because Radrians evolved in environments where temperatures can become extreme, controlling body heat is essential. These organs can therefore vary in size and shape while remaining connected to the same biological function.

DIGESTION

Radrians are strictly carnivorous. Their digestive system contains two stomachs, alongside a muscular gizzard-like organ that can contain swallowed stones known as gastroliths. Their teeth are adapted for tearing rather than grinding.

This means that a Radrian's diet and food preparation can look very different from a human one.

REPRODUCTION

Radrians are ovoviviparous. Their young develop inside eggs that remain within the parent's body until they are ready to hatch.

Radrian females are sometimes referred to as cows, while males may be referred to as bulls. Despite those terms, Radrians are not cattle. They are their own species.

And, importantly for character design: Radrian females do not naturally have human-like breasts. This is also explicitly reflected in the existing species FAQ.

SUBSPECIES

Three Ways of Life

Radrians are divided into three primary subspecies: Southern, Mountain and Forest, shaped by adaptation to different environments.

Radrians are divided into three primary subspecies. Their differences developed largely through adaptation to different environments. These are not three completely separate species. They are different branches of the same Radrian lineage.

Southern: 330-370 cm

Mountain: 340-390 cm

Forest: 290-330 cm

SOUTHERN RADRIAN - THE TRAVELLERS

The Southern Radrian is the most common subspecies. They inhabit the warmer southern regions, particularly the cold deserts and open landscapes of Esterasul. They tend to be tall and relatively slender, with smaller ears and more modest horns.

Their communities are strongly associated with travel. Many Southern Radrians migrate according to the seasons, moving toward warmer regions during winter and cooler areas during summer. This lifestyle shaped much of their culture. They became traders, craftspeople, travellers and scavengers of knowledge.

Their camps and communities are often temporary, allowing them to follow resources, weather and prey.

MOUNTAIN RADRIAN - THE GIANTS

Mountain Radrians are the largest of the three major subspecies. They inhabit the harsh northern mountain regions. Their bodies are powerful and robust, allowing them to survive difficult terrain, steep slopes and extreme weather.

They often possess larger ears, larger horns, stronger bodies and heavier builds. Despite their imposing appearance, Mountain Radrians are traditionally known for their calm and gentle temperament. Some communities maintain semi-permanent settlements rather than following a completely nomadic lifestyle.

FOREST RADRIAN - THE RARE ONES

Forest Radrians inhabit the dense northern forests. They are the smallest major subspecies. They tend to be more compact and muscular, giving them a very different silhouette from Southern Radrians.

They are energetic and often more wary of outsiders. Their horns can develop particularly unusual forms, with pronged horns being a common male trait. Forest environments reward stealth, awareness and agility. Their communities therefore developed differently from the open-land travelling cultures of the south.

THEY ARE STILL RADRIANS

The subspecies aren't rigid personality classes. A Southern Radrian doesn't have to be friendly. A Mountain Radrian doesn't have to be calm. A Forest Radrian doesn't have to be aggressive.

These are tendencies shaped by environment and culture, not personality restrictions. Your character is still an individual.

ESTERASUL

The Original World

A world of cold deserts, high mountains and ancient life. Esterasul is the original home of the Radrians, and it is still being written.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Esterasul is the original home of the Radrians. It is a harsh planet dominated by enormous expanses of cold desert, with forests, mountains and human settlements breaking up the landscape.

The environment is demanding. Water is scarce. Temperatures fluctuate dramatically. Large mountain ranges create their own climates. And across the planet, life has adapted to conditions that would be difficult for humans to survive.

THE COLD DESERTS

The deserts of Esterasul are not necessarily hot. Many are cold deserts, where dry air and strong winds create an environment of enormous temperature extremes. Vast open plains can stretch for hundreds of kilometres. Rocky outcrops interrupt the sand. Plants grow sparsely. Animals must conserve water.

For Radrians, these regions became the foundation of seasonal migration and nomadic life.

THE MOUNTAINS

Esterasul's mountain ranges dominate parts of the horizon. They create natural barriers between regions and dramatically influence local weather. Snow and rainfall in the highlands feed rivers and other water sources below. Human settlements often developed in sheltered valleys where water could be collected and agriculture was possible.

The northern mountains are particularly important to Radrian culture, providing habitats for Mountain Radrians and forming some of the most extreme environments on the planet.

HUMANS

Humans are not native to Esterasul. Their arrival changed the planet's history. Human settlements developed around reliable water sources, eventually creating farming communities and larger towns.

This brought humans and Radrians into increasing contact. Sometimes through trade. Sometimes through cooperation. Sometimes through conflict. The relationship between the two species is therefore an important part of Esterasul's modern history.

RADRIAN CULTURE

There isn't one single Radrian culture. Different clans, regions and subspecies have their own traditions. Many communities are organized around relatively small groups, with larger clans forming when circumstances require it.

Leadership traditionally tends to favour older members of the community, particularly respected women, while shamans and other spiritual figures may hold important positions. Music, dance and oral tradition play major roles.

Radrian communication also relies heavily on body language. Their speech can be accompanied by movements of the ears, tail, head and posture. For more, see the Culture page.

THE NOMADS

Many Radrians traditionally live a semi-nomadic lifestyle. Instead of constructing enormous permanent settlements, they may use tents, temporary structures and communal shelters that can be dismantled or relocated.

This makes sense for a species living in a world where food, water and weather conditions can change dramatically between seasons. Their homes are therefore not simply buildings. Their community travels with them.

A WORLD STILL BEING WRITTEN

Esterasul is not finished. And that's intentional. The original project has always been a long-term worldbuilding project, with new creatures, locations, cultures and ideas continuing to be developed. I always explicitly invited community members to contribute their own creatures and assets to the wider setting.

So when you create something new, you aren't necessarily stepping outside the world. You can become part of its development.

YOUR RADRIAN. YOUR STORY.

The most important thing to understand about Radrians is this: there is a canon, but there is also freedom.

You can explore the original world of Esterasul. You can ignore it completely. You can create a character who lives somewhere else. You can follow the established cultures. You can invent your own. You can make a serious worldbuilding project. Or you can simply draw a funny guy with a long tail. All of those things are valid ways to enjoy Radrians.

The species exists to give you a foundation. What you build on that foundation is up to you.

A Note on Open Design

Esterasul is a collaborative world. Radrians can be designed by anyone following the core traits outlined above. Subspecies, colours, horn shapes, and regional influences are free to mix across regions - migration and mixed heritage are both canon. Use the Subspecies page for regional starting points and the Anatomy section above for structural reference.
