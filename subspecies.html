Regional Variants

Radrian populations drifted apart as clans settled across Esterasul's biomes. The six variants below remain the most distinct, though mixed heritage between neighbouring clans is common and entirely canon. Click any entry to expand its full description.

❈
The Emberwood

Forest Radrian

The most widespread variant, with a dense umber coat and broad, leaf-shaped antler tines that shed rainwater and scatter light beneath the canopy.

Coat: umber & moss green · Crown: broad, leaf-tined · Build: sturdy, quadruped-leaning
＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Forest clans settle beneath the darkest parts of the Emberwood canopy, where their umber coats fade almost completely into the shadow of the fir trunks. Their crowns branch wide and flat, an adaptation that lets rain run harmlessly off the antler rather than pooling in the tines the way it does on narrower crown shapes.

Because so little light reaches the forest floor, Forest Radrians rely heavily on scent and hearing over sight, and their szałasy shelters are built low and half-buried into natural hollows between roots. Clans here are the largest and most stable of any subspecies, sometimes holding the same territory for well over a century.

The Hollow Peaks

Mountain Radrian

Compact and thick-coated, with short, blunt-tined crowns built for headbutting through snow rather than display. The most solitary of the clans.

Coat: grey & slate blue · Crown: short, blunted · Build: low centre of gravity
＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Mountain Radrians trade the sweeping crowns of their lowland cousins for something far more practical: a short, thickened rack strong enough to shoulder through packed snow and loose scree without splintering. Their compact frames and low centre of gravity make them the most sure-footed of any subspecies on the sheer trails of the Hollow Peaks.

Clans here are small, often no more than a family group, and territorial by necessity — the caves that give the Hollow Peaks their name are scarce and fiercely defended. Mountain clans are the only Radrians known to actively compete with other fauna, most notably the Stonehide Boar, for shelter.
