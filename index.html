THE RADRIANS

Living Ecosystems

Radrians are woven into every biome of Esterasul, from the black firs of the Emberwood to the salt-bleached cliffs of the Sunreach Coast. No two clans share quite the same silhouette.

Evolutionary Diversity

Thousands of years of migration and adaptation shaped six major subspecies, each carrying its own antler crown, coat, and quiet customs.

Preservation Through Knowledge

This archive exists to document and preserve the living history of the Radrian species, and to help new keepers design Radrians of their own.

The Marrowfen Migration

Field notes confirm a seasonal migration route linking the fen clans to the Hollow Peaks foothills.

Crown Regrowth Rates

New measurements suggest antler crowns regrow nearly twice as fast after a harsh winter.

The Glassfrost Vocal Range

Tundra Radrians produce a low resonant call rarely recorded in any other population.

Shared Shelter Behaviour
