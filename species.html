A Note on Open Design

Esterasul is a collaborative world. Radrians can be designed by anyone following the core traits outlined above, subspecies, colours, horn shapes are free to mix across regions - migration and mixed heritage are both canon. Use the Subspecies page for regional starting points and the Anatomy page for structural reference.
