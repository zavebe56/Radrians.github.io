OVERVIEW

The Radrians are an original, open species native to the forests, peaks, and deserts of Esterasul. Every Radrian carries horns unique to their bloodline, a coat suited to their home region, and a quiet, clannish way of life built around shared shelters. This page collects the essentials — origin, classification, society, and the design principles that keep every Radrian recognisable.

TERMINOLOGY

Words Worth Knowing

Szałasy — clan shelters. Crown — the antler structure. Clans are typically named for their home region.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Szałasy refers to the shelters a clan builds and rebuilds on the same ground across generations — half structure, half heirloom. Crown is the standard term for a Radrian's antler structure, used instead of "antlers" throughout this archive to reflect how central it is to identity.

Clans are typically named for the region they call home — Forestclan, Peakclan, Fenclan, Shoreclan, Frostclan, and Duneclan being the most common groupings, though mixed-heritage clans near region borders sometimes carry compound names of their own.

DESIGN GUIDE

Making Your Own

As an open species, anyone may design a Radrian. Keep the three core traits, then let region, personality, and lineage decide the rest.

＋ Read full entry Collapse entry

Designing a Radrian starts with the three core traits — a crown, digitigrade hindlegs, and a clan bond — and everything past that is a choice. Picking a home region first (see the Subspecies page) is the fastest way to land on a coherent coat, build, and crown shape, though mixed heritage across two neighbouring regions is entirely canon.

From there, personality and lineage are free to shape the rest: unusual crown patterns, scars, off-palette coats, and other individual quirks are all welcome as long as the three core traits stay intact.

A Note on Open Design

Esterasul is a collaborative world. Radrians can be designed by anyone following the core traits outlined above, and clans, colours, and crown shapes are free to mix across regions — migration and mixed heritage are both canon. Use the Subspecies page for regional starting points and the Anatomy page for structural reference.
